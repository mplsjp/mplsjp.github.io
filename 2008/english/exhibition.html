ITOCHU Techno-Solutions Corporation

In CTC booth, you can watch the demonstrations of "PATHMANAGER", MPLS-TE network provisioning solution.

This product, which has been continuously developed by Cloud-Scope Technologies,ARIA Networks and CTC, supports Telecoms to achieve productivity improvement and operational cost reduction.
MPLS-TE technology has some operational problems, ex., TE-LSP must be designed manually, it is difficult to design routes with consideration protection bandwidth, although it has various benefits like higher resiliency, reliability and granular traffic management, etc. Our solution offers not only TE-LSP configuration to actual nodes and routes visualization in MPLS-TE network, but also automatic and optimized TE-LSP designing by using "iVNT", path computation product of ARIA Networks.
You would also watch fault management demonstration of PATHMANAGER at Cloud-Scope booth.
